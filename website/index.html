Is Cinedor free?

Is it safe to install?

Download Cinedor only from this website. The app checks that it is the official copy and updates itself securely.

Which devices are supported?

Android phones and tablets with Android 7.0 or newer. iPhone is not supported yet.

That is normal for apps outside the Play Store. Tap Settings, allow installs from your browser, then go back and tap Install.
