FAQ

Is Cinedor free?

Yes. You can watch for free. An optional Premium removes ads.

Is it safe to install?

Download Cinedor only from this website. The app checks that it is the official copy and updates itself securely from inside the app.

Which phones are supported?

Android 7.0 or newer. iPhone is not supported yet.

Android says “blocked” or “unknown app” — what now?

That is normal for apps outside the Play Store. Tap Settings, allow installs from your browser or file manager, then go back and tap Install.

How do I update?

When a new version is out, Cinedor shows an Update available window. Tap Update now — your downloads and My List stay.

How do I report a problem?

Open Cinedor → Me → Feedback & help. We reply there and tell you when it is fixed.
